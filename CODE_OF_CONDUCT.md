# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Aurontra community a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue content, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may take appropriate temporary or permanent action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to **hello@tinlance.com** with a concise description and relevant links. Do not use this address for security vulnerability reports; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Maintainers should acknowledge reports within five business days and keep details confidential to the extent practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly, communicate decisions where appropriate, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of reporter or affected-person information. If a report concerns the person responsible for enforcement, contact the repository owner through GitHub and identify the conflict.
